Piraeus Review: personal application terms

Updated 2026-11-14. Contact: akumanara@gmail.com.

This application is for its owner's own accounts and personal, noncommercial financial review. It is not a public service and does not accept accounts from other people. The owner must follow the bank's and Enable Banking's applicable terms and authorize only accounts they are entitled to access.

Functionality

The app reads account information, calculates cash-flow summaries, stores local reports and optionally requests AI commentary on the month's transactions. It does not initiate payments, transfers, investments or changes to bank accounts. Local categorization corrections alter the local ledger only.

Interpreting reports

Results depend on imported history, bank availability, account coverage and classification accuracy. The owner should reconcile reports against bank records. Cash flow does not establish net worth, savings, tax obligations or investment suitability. AI commentary can be wrong and should be reviewed before making decisions.

Operation

Scheduled use requires the owner's computer or chosen host to be available, credentials to remain valid and consent to be renewed when required. The owner controls installation, storage, model selection, any API costs and removal of the app.

Read the privacy notice for this app's data handling.
