Piraeus Review: personal app privacy notice

Updated 2026-11-14. Contact: akumanara@gmail.com.

This is a private, noncommercial application operated by its account owner to review their own finances. It is not open to other users and is not a bank service.

Account information

The owner authorizes Enable Banking to retrieve selected bank-account balances and booked transactions. Authentication takes place through the bank and provider authorization flow. This application does not collect a bank password or request payment initiation.

Local storage

Account identifiers needed for synchronization, authorization-session information, transactions, current balance snapshots, categorization rules and reports are kept in a local data folder controlled by the owner. Application files are not encrypted by this software; the owner is responsible for device protection, backups and full-disk encryption. Files remain until the owner deletes them. Revoking a bank session does not delete existing reports or the ledger.

Optional AI commentary

AI is disabled by default. When the owner explicitly selects OpenAI mode, the month's totals, spending categories, merchant names, transaction descriptions, dates and amounts, account names and balance snapshots are sent to the OpenAI API. Account and card numbers are removed, and bank credentials, access tokens and bank identifiers are never included. API response storage is disabled, but the API provider's other processing and retention rules still apply. A local-model option sends the same data to an Ollama server on the owner's computer; the owner controls that server and model configuration. The AI has no tools and cannot act on any account.

Other services

Enable Banking and the bank process information needed to provide authorized account access under their own terms. The selected AI provider, if enabled, processes that data under its terms. This app does not send email or publish the owner's financial reports. Publishing these static information pages does not publish bank data.

Control

The owner can stop scheduled jobs, revoke account access, disable optional AI, and remove local files and backups. Provider-held copies are subject to the provider's policies. Questions about this private app can be directed to the contact above.
